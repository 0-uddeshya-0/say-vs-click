Information and consent

You are invited to take part in a research study about how people evaluate answers produced by an AI research assistant.

  • You will complete seven short tasks (one practice, then six main trials). In each, a factual claim is shown together with the assistant's answer and its cited sources.
  • Your task is to judge whether the answer is supported by the cited sources, then rate your confidence and trust.
  • Please note: some answers, reasoning steps, or sources may contain inaccuracies.
  • Your responses and interaction behaviour (e.g. which sources you open, timing, scrolling) are recorded for research purposes.
  • Data are pseudonymous — linked only to your Prolific ID — and used solely for academic research. We do not store your IP address.
  • Participation is voluntary. You may stop at any time by closing this page; partial data may be retained for analysis.
  • The session takes about 10–15 minutes.

Your task

We are evaluating the quality of a new AI research assistant's answers. You will review a series of short factual claims, each paired with the assistant's answer.

  1. Read the claim and the answer. Some answers also show the assistant's step-by-step reasoning.
  2. Check the sources if you wish. Answers cite numbered sources — click a chip like [1] to open the source excerpt. You can open and close sources as often as you like.
  3. Give your verdict — is the answer supported by the cited sources? Then rate your confidence and how much you trust the answer.

There is one practice trial first, then six main trials. Please work at a natural pace and base each verdict on the sources as you see them.
